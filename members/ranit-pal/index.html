Drummer • Percussionist

Ranit Pal

Who He Is
The energy source behind the band’s movement. Ranit is the one holding the chaos together with precision, aggression, and timing. His drumming doesn’t just keep tempo; it drives emotion and momentum.
Signature Style
  • Tight, energetic drumming with explosive fills
  • Strong rhythmic control with creative groove patterns
  • Ability to shift between subtle dynamics and full-stage intensity instantly
Legacy & Vision
Deeply interested in technology and technical systems, Ranit brings an analytical mindset into rhythm and live performance. His precision and consistency become the foundation that allows the rest of the band to experiment freely.

He’s also the reason rehearsals occasionally become louder than intended.
