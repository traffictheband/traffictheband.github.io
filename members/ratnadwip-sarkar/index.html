Ratnadwip Sarkar

Who He Is
The sonic thinker of the band. Ratnadwip focuses on textures, atmosphere, tone, and the tiny details most people don’t notice consciously, but definitely feel. Whether it’s building guitar layers, shaping synth textures, or discussing live sound setups for hours, he constantly pushes the band toward a more immersive sound.
Signature Style
  • Atmospheric and groove-focused guitar riffs
  • Layered sound design elements blended into live instrumentation
  • Smooth transitions between rhythm, melody, and ambient textures
Legacy & Vision
With a strong background in sound engineering, music production, and technology-driven creativity, Ratnadwip helps bridge the gap between musical performance and sonic experience. He loves experimenting with synths, pedals, textures, and unconventional ideas that give Traffic its modern edge.

Also known for randomly noodling on guitar during breaks until someone says, “Wait, play that again.”
