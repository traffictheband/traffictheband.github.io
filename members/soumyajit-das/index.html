Soumyajit Das

Who He Is
The architect of emotion and arrangement inside the band. Soumyajit brings theatrical energy into music, turning performances into experiences. His understanding of storytelling, dynamics, and stagecraft helps Traffic connect deeply with audiences.
Signature Style
  • Complex musical arrangements with emotional layering
  • Powerful and versatile vocals
  • Dynamic guitar work with expressive stage movement
Legacy & Vision
Coming from a drama background, Soumyajit understands the psychology of performance. He doesn’t just sing songs; he performs them. From crowd interaction to emotional delivery, he knows how to make the audience feel involved rather than just watching from afar.

He’s also the person most likely to turn a simple rehearsal into a full cinematic concept.
