Guitarist

Sumit Shaw

Who He Is
The groove keeper and texture builder. Sumit’s playing adds warmth, movement, and feel to Traffic’s sound. His rhythm work often becomes the invisible glue that makes songs feel catchy, smooth, and alive.
Signature Style
  • Tight rhythm sections with tasteful groove
  • Catchy melodic licks and clean transitions
  • Strong understanding of musical feel over unnecessary complexity
Legacy & Vision
With deep knowledge of string instruments and musical phrasing, Sumit brings balance to the band’s sound. He understands when to step forward and when to let the music breathe, a rare skill in any guitarist.

Usually quiet. Until someone hands him a guitar.
