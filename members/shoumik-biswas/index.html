Lead Vocalist • Bassist • Songwriter

Shoumik Biswas

Who He Is
The kind of performer who naturally commands attention the moment he walks onto a stage. Shoumik carries the soul of the band through powerful vocals and bass grooves that glue the entire rhythm section together. Whether it's an emotional ballad or an energetic rock anthem, he knows exactly how to make a crowd feel the song.
Signature Style
  • Deep, groovy bass lines with strong rhythmic control
  • Soulful vocals that can move from blues-inspired warmth to explosive high-energy sections
  • Emotional phrasing and natural stage charisma
Legacy & Vision
With previous television appearances and strong live performance experience, Shoumik understands performance beyond just music. His deep understanding of acoustic instruments and songwriting helps shape the emotional core of Traffic’s sound.

Off-stage, he’s the calm force in the middle of musical chaos, until rehearsal starts.
